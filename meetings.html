The sociocracy initiative

Sociocracy is a collaborative governance method that ensures everyone's voice is included.

It helps organizations like ours make decisions more efficiently and inclusively. Adopting this model means more transparency and equal participation, paving the way for innovative solutions and enhanced community engagement.

PSAD began this journey in 2024 with 52 Alliance. Your involvement shapes it: attend the trainings, share your feedback, and help develop the model.

Summary report from the May 11, 2024 development session.

The journey in 2024

  1. Board introductionThe discussion started in February.
  2. Member introductionMarch 23, at the general meeting, with details sent by email.
  3. In-depth trainingMay 11. A full day of learning and practice with Katie Murch of 52 Alliance.
  4. Feedback and ideasMay 18, at the general meeting.
  5. Summer developmentSessions to refine the approach based on member feedback.
  6. Final voteSeptember 21, at the general meeting.
