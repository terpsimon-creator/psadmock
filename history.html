126 years, still counting.

PSAD's story started on July 4, 1900, with a picnic on Mercer Island, where 35 people gathered to form a society for Deaf people in the Salish Sea region. It is one of the oldest Deaf clubs in the world.

  1. 1900

    The Seattle Deaf Mutes Society

    Established in 1900 as the “Seattle Deaf Mutes Society.” The following year, the organization changed its name to the Puget Sound Association of the Deaf (PSAD), with the primary goal of providing community and support for D/DB/HH individuals.

  2. 1901

    July 4

    The first picnic

    The first official PSAD gathering was a picnic on Mercer Island. The celebration brought in PSAD's first income: $10.10.

  3. 1904

    Recognized in law

    PSAD was recognized as an organization in law.

  4. 1911

    Monthly meetings begin

    PSAD began meeting once a month, with an initiation fee of $1 and membership dues of 50 cents. They met in a rented hall that cost $6 a month.

  5. 1926

    Incorporated as a nonprofit

    PSAD's official nonprofit incorporation in law.

  6. 1930

    July 4

    The 30th anniversary picnic

  7. 1941

    Deaf workers at Boeing

    The association is proud to recognize the many Deaf Washingtonians who worked at Boeing during World War II. They earned less than one dollar an hour.

  8. 1953

    What dues cost

    Membership dues were $1.50 per man and $1 per woman.

  9. 1961

    May 28

    ASL goes underwater

    Dr. Peter Wisher came up with the idea of teaching U.S. Navy frogmen, known today as Navy SEALs, to use ASL underwater as a communication advantage.

  10. 1963

    October

    Saved in court

    PSAD was about to be shut down by a lawsuit. Jack and Jane Kinney and Robert and Dorothy Kinney stepped in, appeared in court, and asked for the case to be dropped.

  11. 1975

    April 19

    The 75th anniversary banquet

    Held at the Hilton hotel in Seattle.

  12. 1975

    May 29

    A day at the warm springs

    PSAD members hanging out at a warm springs area.

  13. 1975

    December 20

    A house of our own

    PSAD purchased a house in Ballard, social hall included. Kudos to Carter & Kinney and the PSAD members who made the purchase happen.

  14. 1980s

    A new logo

    A PSAD member created a new logo for the association.

  15. 1983

    Raising the house

    The PSAD house went through a major remodel, including lifting the house 24 inches to make room for a social hall for meetings, gatherings, and events, while renting out the upstairs apartment. Member Everett Hollebeck led the team and the project.

  16. 1994

    What dues cost

    Membership dues were $10 per person or $15 per couple.

  17. 1996

    A social at PSAD Hall

  18. 2006

    The PSAD officers

  19. 2007

    Spring

    Members remodel the interior

    PSAD members volunteered to do a major interior remodel. It took them two and a half months to complete.

  20. 2008

    April

    A trip to China

    PSAD members went on a trip to China.

  21. 2014

    July 1

    First time at NAD

    PSAD was represented at the National Association of the Deaf (NAD) for the first time.

  22. 2015

    Summer

    The community picnic

  23. 2016

    September

    Deaf Thrive Day

    PSAD is proud to be one of the founding members and the fiscal sponsor of the Deaf Thrive Day festival. President Will Roach represented the association's booth at the event in South Lake Union.

  24. 2017

    Winter

    Ski and snowboard camp

    PSAD partnered with Outdoors for All to host a Deaf-led ski and snowboard camp at Stevens Pass for D/HH kids.

  25. 2019

    The Deaf chefs dinner

    A gourmet course dinner at PSAD, prepared and cooked by professional Deaf chefs Kurt Ramborger and Nick Aebly and their assistant chef, Ocean.

  26. 2021

    Spring

    ASL-friendly cornhole

    PSAD sponsored ASL-friendly cornhole leagues.

  27. 2022

    June 19

    Juneteenth block party

    PSAD hosted a Juneteenth celebration block party with three performers: Miki Smith, Chad Ervin, and Sheilyn Delacruz.

  28. 2022

    July

    BBQ and disc golf

    PSAD partnered with the Washington Deaf Disc Golf Association (WDDGA) to host a BBQ and disc golf tournament at Silver Lake Park.

  29. 2022

    November 16

    A new website

    PSAD launched a completely redesigned website by association webmaster Perseus McDaniel, built to preserve and showcase PSAD's long legacy and to attract future members and supporters.

  30. 2023

    Summer

    Upgrading the apartment

    PSAD upgraded the apartment at the house ahead of a new tenant.

  31. 2024

    May 11

    Learning sociocracy together

    Members spent a full day in a sociocracy workshop with Katie Murch of 52 Alliance, exploring a more collaborative way to govern PSAD. Read about the initiative.

  32. 2026

    September 27

    125 years

    PSAD marked its 125th anniversary at the Mercer Island Community & Event Center, on the island where the first picnic was held. See the celebration.
