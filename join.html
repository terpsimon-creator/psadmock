What members get

Network with diverse communities of Deaf, DeafBlind, Deaf+, hard of hearing, and late-deafened people locally, nationally, and internationally.

  • A vote in PSAD elections and decisions
  • Discounted PSAD event prices
  • Invitations to special PSAD events
  • The opportunity to serve on PSAD's board or a committee
  • PSAD's newsletter with PNW Deaf E-News and more
  • Social and leadership skills development
  • Opportunities to interact with members at events
  • First to know about PSAD events

There is an open seat on the board.
