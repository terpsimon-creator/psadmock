125 years, back where it began.

On September 27, 2026, PSAD marked its 125th anniversary at the Mercer Island Community & Event Center, on the island where the first picnic was held. Generations came together to honor the past, celebrate the present, and support the future of PSAD. The celebration sold out.

The anniversary dinner

Head Chef Kurt Ramborger. Sous Chef Daniel Beool.

Starters

Classic Caesar Salad
Quarter-cut romaine with house-made Caesar dressing, garlicky croutons, and a parmesan crisp roll.
“1905” Columbia Salad
Iceberg lettuce, julienne smoked ham, julienne Swiss cheese, halved grape tomatoes, smashed Castelvetrano olives, and slivered red onion, finished with grated Pecorino and tossed in a garlic-Worcestershire vinaigrette.

Entrées

Chicken
Lemony airline chicken with brown and wild rice and honey-glazed carrots.
Beef
Grilled steak with gratin de pommes, mashed cauliflower, blistered haricots verts, and demi-glace.
Vegetarian
“Veggie Delights” quinoa cake, breaded tofu, sliced beet carpaccio, and grilled portobello mushroom, finished with a ToMinTo vinaigrette.

Sweets

Chef Daniel's Tiramisu
Chef Kurt's Flourless Chocolate Torte
